# Transitions and camera direction

## Choose a carrier

A carrier is the element whose continuity makes adjacent beats feel like one film. Prefer a stable center or intentional trajectory, a morphable silhouette, explicit geometry, enough layering to hide face changes, and no dependency on layout reflow.

Reusable chains include frame -> badge -> device, chip -> prompt -> workspace, or chart mark -> logo. Do not reuse one chain for every film.

## Morph

1. Resolve outgoing content.
2. Begin carrier geometry travel.
3. Reveal the incoming face while moving or once silhouettes match.
4. Set stale faces to `display: none` afterward.
5. Clear incompatible filters, backdrop filters, shadows, and transforms at the destination.

Opacity may clean internal faces. A disappearing source followed by an appearing destination is not a morph.

## Match-cut

Match center, dimensions, outline, travel direction, velocity, visual weight, and dominant color. Swap at the closest silhouette match and continue motion through it so the eye follows velocity.

## Particle-reassemble

- Emit particles from the visible source coordinates.
- Inherit source color or energy.
- Use controlled variation rather than uniform random noise.
- Persist long enough to show cause and effect.
- Converge toward the next object or focal zone when reassembly is promised.

Keep the particle stage independent when it must survive the source shell. Use a few depth groups with different scale, blur, and travel instead of many identical dots.

## Camera direction

- Push in to make typing, a control, or result legible.
- Pan only when the destination contains new information.
- Pull back to reveal context or conclude proof.
- Hold the destination long enough to inspect.

Avoid a push in, snap back, and second push. Maintain close framing through a prompt-to-workspace morph, then use one slow pullback. Transform one stable camera or shell layer; only scale nested layers for deliberate parallax.

## Boundary review

Scrub before, during, and after every handoff. Confirm no blank frame, stale flash, abrupt shadow/filter change, text-width jump, competing sentences, unexplained transform reset, or incoming content detached from its carrier.
